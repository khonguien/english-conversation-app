# BỘ HỘI THOẠI TÌNH HUỐNG NHÀ HÀNG (COMPLETE RESTAURANT ROLEPLAY DIALOGUES)

Tài liệu này tổng hợp và liên kết toàn bộ các mẫu câu từ video và tài liệu thực tế thành chuỗi 6 đoạn hội thoại nhập vai (roleplay) hoàn chỉnh, từ lúc bước vào nhà hàng cho tới khi thanh toán và rời đi.

---

## Phần 1: Đón Customer, Đặt bàn & Chọn chỗ ngồi (Arrival, Reservation & Seating)

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Hi, how may I help you? How many people are you guys?"

👤 **Customer:** "Hi, a table for two, please. Do you have a table available right now?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Did you have a reservation with us?"

👤 **Customer:** "Yes, I have a reservation for two at 7 PM under the name John."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Let me check that for you right away. Yes, Mr. John, your table is all set! Right this way, please."

👤 **Customer:** "Could we get a table by the window, please? We'd prefer a quiet table if possible."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Let me check if that table is reserved; if not, you're welcome to move. Yes, it's free! Right here."

👤 **Customer:** "This table is great, thank you. And non-smoking area, please."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "All of our indoor seating is non-smoking. Here are your menus. I'll give you a few minutes to look them over."

👤 **Customer:** "This is perfect, thank you."

---

## Phần 2: Hỏi gợi ý thực đơn (Asking for Recommendations)

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Hi, what can I get for you guys today? Are you ready to order?"

👤 **Customer:** "Excuse me, can we order now? But first, what do you recommend? What's your most popular dish here?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Our ribeye steak and seafood pasta are definitely our top best-sellers."

👤 **Customer:** "What's the dish of the day? What would you personally choose?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Today's special is grilled salmon served with asparagus. Personally, that's my absolute favorite."

👤 **Customer:** "That sounds fantastic! Is that dish gluten-free?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Yes, that dish is completely gluten-free."

👤 **Customer:** "Awesome. We'll take a minute to decide our main courses."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Take your time, we're in no rush at all."

---

## Phần 3: Gọi món chi tiết & Tùy chỉnh (Full Course Ordering & Customizing)

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Welcome back! Are you ready to place your order?"

👤 **Customer:** "Yes, we're ready. For the appetizers, let me get the chicken wings as well as the French fries, please."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Fantastic choice. And what would you like for the main course?"

👤 **Customer:** "Let me get the ribeye steak with a side of mashed potatoes, please."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Great choice! And how would you like your steak cooked?"

👤 **Customer:** "Let me get that medium rare, please."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Understood: ribeye steak, medium rare with mashed potatoes. And for your guest?"

👤 **Customer:** "I'd like the grilled chicken pasta, but could you make it less spicy, please? And no onions, please."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Noted: grilled chicken pasta, less spicy, no onions. Would you like the dressing on the side?"

👤 **Customer:** "Yes, extra cheese, and dressing on the side, please."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Ok. And what would you like to drink?"

👤 **Customer:** "Can I just get a glass of red wine, and a bottle of mineral water, please?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Sure, no problem! I'll be right back with your drinks and appetizers."

---

## Phần 4: Trong bữa ăn & Xử lý sự cố (During the Meal & Handling Issues)

👤 **Customer:** "Excuse me, could we get some cutlery? And can I get another napkin, please?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Right away, here is a fresh set of cutlery and napkins for you."

👤 **Customer:** "Could we also have some more water and extra bread, please?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Certainly, I'll have the kitchen prepare another basket of warm bread for you."

*(Vài phút sau khi món chính được dọn ra)*

👤 **Customer:** "Excuse me, I think this is not what I ordered. There seems to be a mistake with my order, and the food is cold."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "I am so sorry about that! Let me check the ticket for you immediately. Apologies for the inconvenience."

👤 **Customer:** "Could you please replace this with a warm, fresh one?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Certainly! We will have the kitchen prepare a fresh replacement right away. We'll get it out as soon as possible."

---

## Phần 5: Đánh giá món ăn & Món tráng miệng (Food Review & Dessert)

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Hi, how was everything? How is everything tasting so far?"

👤 **Customer:** "The food was excellent! The steak was truly delicious and cooked to perfection. Thank you very much."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "I'm so glad to hear that! Fantastic. Would you like to order any dessert?"

👤 **Customer:** "No, I'm stuffed, thank you!"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Would you like any coffee or tea, or would you like to pick a light dessert?"

👤 **Customer:** "Actually, just surprise me with your best dessert to share."

🛎️ **Waiter:** "You got it! I'll bring out our house specialty dessert right away."

---

## Phần 6: Hóa đơn, Gói mang về & Thanh toán (Bill, Leftovers To-Go & Payment)

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Is there anything else I can help you guys with?"

👤 **Customer:** "No, we're all good, thank you. Can I just get the bill and a to-go box for these leftovers?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Of course. I'll just leave the bill right here, and I'll be right back with that to-go box."

*(Bồi bàn mang hộp lại)*

👤 **Customer:** "Could you split the bill between us?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Sure, would you like to split it evenly down the middle?"

👤 **Customer:** "Yes, please. Can I pay by card?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Yes, we accept all major cards. You can tap or insert right here."

👤 **Customer:** "Is service included in the total?"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Yes, a service charge is already included in the total."

👤 **Customer:** "Great. Keep the change as an extra tip for your wonderful service today!"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "That's very kind of you, thank you so much! Thank you for dining with us today!"

👤 **Customer:** "Thank you, the meal was wonderful! Have a great day!"

🛎️ **Waiter:** "Thank you! Have a wonderful day ahead, hope to see you again soon!"
